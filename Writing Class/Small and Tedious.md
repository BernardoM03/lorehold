
Diogo felt the wet blades of grass bristle on his arm as he laid atop a windy hill. The weather was unusually warm, especially for the early winter season. The fog rolling from the shores was still there of course, but the clouds in the sky parted to let some of Lathander's light through.

Diogo sat up stiffly. and clasped his hands, stretching them as far back as he could. There were a few cracks, but his posture immediately relaxed. He could see whole town of Kibton from up here. 

He could also see the stern expression and hanging empty bottle of Ser Alric at the bottom of the hill. The rider scowled and turned to walk back to the boarding house.

Diogo groaned and pushed himself up to his feet. He began to brush himself off as he felt the clump of mud stick to his hand.

Last night's rain stayed with the earth, and the muddy soil stuck itself across Diogo's back. Diogo started to take a deep breath, but interrupted himself as he clasped the collar of his shirt off and pulled as hard as he could. It tore straight down the middle.

He roared and began to march down to the boarding house. He passed through few confused villagers of Kibton. One mother covered her daughter's eyes from looking at Diogo's immodest appearance.

Diogo entered into the boarding house. Ser Alric was sitting on a long table, talking to other drunkards he met last night.

"What's the face for lad. Why don't you go grab us another round of drinks, huh?" spoke one of the ugly drunk bastards.

Diogo ignored him and looked to his master. "Didya need something Ser?" 

The drunkard stood up right before Diogo and thrusted his hand to his throat, pushing him towards a pillar. Diogo's breath had left his lungs upon the impact. He gasped what little he could still breathe. 

"Don' you ignore me whoreson," said the man. He squeezed his grip, and looked the boy up and down. He grinned and looked back to the table. "How bout you squire under me. I can teach you things that ol' dog can't do anymore," licking his lips. 

The table erupted in laughter, except for the Ol' Dog. He had a blank stare pointed at Diogo and the man. His face was pale. He kept silent for a moment before life once again breathed through him. He scowled again, looking the boy in front of him up and down. "You look shit." He sat in place.

Diogo's throat constricted with each passing second. He looked to his master with his worsening condition, and his eyes opened in inspiration. Diogo started to take one last deep breath and found enough to speak.

"Na-rrow... Strait...," letting the last of his breath out. Ser Alric looked to the grappled boy, and smiled widely, bursting into a chorus of laughter. 

"THAT'S ALL YOU HAD TO SAY BOY," he proclaimed to the whole room. He stood up in a flash, gripping the sheathed sword at his belt. In a swift motion he threw the scabbard to clatter upon the floorboards, letting the blinding reflection room's torches swing around the him. The melody of steel and laughter hummed, crescendoing until it met with flesh.

Diogo dropped to the floor and immediately kicked the corpse away from him. The hand was no longer around him but his throat still did not open. He looked up and saw the figure of his master stand above him. He walked to the door of the boarding house, looked back to Diogo, and scowled.
