"You've got to be fucking kidding me."

The Elder Monk was as unflinching as the limestone statue behind him. "You are to be gone before night's fall." 

"Elder, please..." Diogo pleaded through his clenched teeth. "I've only a few days before I finish with the library texts. After that, I'll have the notes I need and you'll never see me again."

Unflinching. "I am afraid you don't understand. The light of knowledge is not something that can be shown to sinners like you. There is no exchange. You had a privilege, and have now lost it." 

The Elder Monk's voice was soft and firm, which reminded Diogo of... His eyes shifted beyond the Elder Monk, seeing the meekish monk standing behind him like boy behind his mother's skirt. He clenched his fist until his chest tightened and the ringing got louder. This was beginning to sober him up.

The Elder Monk noticed this. "Do not blame Brother Franco for your vices, Brother Diogo. He confessed his sin and was cleansed by the flame." The monk leaned closer to him. "You have refused, and thus blaspheme the Morninglord himself." His breath faintly smelled of fish.

Diogo pushed the Elder Monk away. "I lost 15 golds to him these two months I've been here and the moment I fucking win and he owes me, now he gets all fucking righteous? He's a sore fucking loser... whose scared." Diogo heaved, his breath becoming coarser and his chest tighter.

Diogo did not notice the Elder Monk's shift from seriousness to worry. He tried to speak at several moments, but was interrupted each time.

Diogo's word began to stumble beneath his harsh breathing, blood with each cough "Let. Me. Tell you. Something, Elder. Only. Reason. He's hiding. Behind. You is. Because. I'd kill him. Not. Because. Of. Your... Precious Morninglord." 

Diogo's attention focused on a small petrel that landed on the red pauldron of the Elder Monk. He took this opportunity to speak, but Diogo could not hear him.

It twitched in place, staring straight at Diogo. It wings were grey like the sea and it's eyes black dots into an endless all consuming void. It squawked, turning it's head to the side like it was confused. Or maybe it really was just as dumb as a bird.

"..ven hear me, Brother Diogo?" said the Elder Monk. Diogo turned for a moment to see that his worry turned to frustration. Turning back, the petrel was gone. He closed his eyes and took in a deep breath, but his cough returned and even more blood was belched.

His mind was clear now. Pain remained of course, but it was clear and- sober.

"I.. Uhm. I'm alright Elder," he responded. The moment was still foggy, as if Diogo entered into a dream. 

"Well, as I was saying Brother Diogo. The tenets of the Morn-." His lecture halted as Diogo raised his hands and thrusted them upon the Elder Monk's chest. Red robes splayed onto the limestone in a thud. 

Diogo scowled down upon the Elder Monk. "I will pick up my things from my room and I will leave you and your monastery alone." He turned around.

"Do not belittle me." Diogo walked away.
