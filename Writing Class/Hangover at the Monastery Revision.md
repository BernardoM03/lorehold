"You've got to be fucking kidding me."

The Elder Monk was as unflinching as the limestone statue behind him. "You are to be gone before night's fall." 

"Elder, please..." Diogo pleaded through his clenched teeth. "I've only a few days before I finish with the library texts. After that, I'll have the notes I need and you'll never see me again."

Unflinching. "I am afraid you don't understand. The light of knowledge is not something that can be shown to sinners like you. There is no exchange. You had a privilege, and have now lost it." 

The Elder Monk's voice was soft and firm, which reminded Diogo of... His eyes shifted beyond the Elder Monk, seeing the meekish monk standing behind him like boy behind his mother's skirt. He clenched his fist until his chest tightened and the ringing got louder. This was beginning to sober him up.

The Elder Monk noticed this. "Do not blame Brother Franco for your vices, Brother Diogo. He confessed his sin and was cleansed by the flame." The monk leaned closer to him. "You have refused, and thus blaspheme the Morninglord himself." His breath faintly smelled of fish.
