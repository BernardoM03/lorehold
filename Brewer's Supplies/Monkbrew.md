Originally of Lathanderite origin, this earthy tea is made of cleavers that are cut, dried, brewed and reduced until as brown as dirt. Monks have noted deeper concentration within meditation after ingestion.

When you drink this brew, you are able to concentrate on spells much easier for the next hour. You gain a bonus to Concentration Saving Throws equal to the quality of this brew.

Standard: +1
Quality (DC 15): +2
Masterwork (DC 25): +3
