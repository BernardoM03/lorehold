Whenever you finish a Long Rest while holding Brewer’s Supplies or an Herbalism Kit, you may use that tool during downtime to create Doses equal to Proficiency Bonus from either the Brew or Medicine list. Additionally, once you complete a short rest, you gain an additional dose of one of your selected creations.

All Doses expire once you complete another long rest and cannot be used anymore.

Doses can be evaluated as Standard, Quality, or Masterwork, which require certain DCs to be met upon creation.
